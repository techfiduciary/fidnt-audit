§3Method (repeat it)

  1. Lay the two project lists side by side (NEP vs House bill).
  2. Sort every project into four piles: unchanged / raised-or-cut / added by the House / removed by the House.
  3. Sum each pile; reconcile against the printed bill totals.
  4. Duplications: exact-amount and same-object review across the bill's own volumes.
  5. Publish provisional results with named, page-cited cases; hold the rest at aggregate until tied to a printed line.

§4Caveats adopted from the commons' own discipline

  • "Not in the NEP" does not prove a project is improper; some are renamed NEP items.
  • Candidate matches and deltas are provisional; unmatched rows are not proof of policy change.
  • The ₱37.8B unplaced money is the measure of the district-inference method's limit.
  • Flags and thresholds are starting points, not editorial standards.
  • Validate every lead against the original documents before filing anything anywhere.
